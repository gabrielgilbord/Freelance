# PRODUCT · COTA

> Product truth for design work. Written on 2026-11-14 from the owner's brief (the owner works from a phone and asked for autonomous decisions; Impeccable's init interview was substituted by the brief and earlier project context).

## What it is
COTA is a one-person software engineering studio. It designs, builds and runs software end to end: web and mobile apps, SaaS platforms, applied AI, IoT and connected systems, software architecture, and business automation and integrations.

## Who it is for
- Companies that need custom software and don't want to coordinate several vendors.
- Founders with a product idea that is not yet defined.
- Businesses that want AI applied to a concrete task, with measurable results.
- Industrial and equipment companies that need IoT.
- Agencies and consultancies that need senior technical capacity.

## What the site must do
Persuade (Impeccable mode: **Persuade**). A visitor should leave the first viewport believing COTA can build a complete technical system, from the device to the interface, with a single accountable engineer, and should want to start a conversation. The primary action is the contact form ("Hablemos de tu proyecto").

## Proof available
- No clients, testimonials or results yet. **Never invent them.**
- Three technical demos, always labelled "Concepto · Demo técnica" and "datos simulados".
- The quality of the site itself, and the illustrations, are the proof of craft.

## Must stay true
- Spanish copy, first person singular.
- Contact form with server validation, honeypot and pluggable delivery.
- SEO: metadata, JSON-LD, sitemap, canonical URLs.
- Accessibility WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion respected.
- Legal pages (aviso legal, privacidad).

## Brand commitments
- Name: COTA. Isotipo: the open "c" closed by the oblique tick of a dimension line.
- Palette pinned by the owner: graphite black, off-white, COTA orange.
- Fonts pinned by the owner: Schibsted Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono.
- The dimension line ("cota") is the graphic motif: from start to end.
